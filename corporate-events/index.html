Corporate Event Planning Services

1
Venue Scouting & Selection

We find the ideal location — or multiple venues — to match the tone and objectives of your corporate event, whether it’s a single-day gathering or a multi-day experience.

2
Travel Coordination

Complete travel management for teams and guests, including flight logistics, private transfers, and local transportation across Italy.

3
Accommodation Management

Booking and coordination of stays for all participants in comfortable, well-located hotels or exclusive villas near the event venue.

4
Catering & Restaurant Selection

Curated dining experiences — from gala banquets to informal lunches — with the best catering partners and Italian cuisine options.

5
Supplier Sourcing & Management

We identify and manage trusted suppliers based on your event needs: audio systems, lighting, photography, staging, floral design, and more.

6
Event Logistics & Transportation

Coordination of buses, vans, or private transfers for smooth movement between venues or activity locations.

7
Timeline & Event Flow Coordination

Creation of a detailed schedule and supervision of every stage, ensuring that timing, flow, and guest experience are perfectly balanced.

8
On-Site Event Management

Our team is present on-site to oversee the setup, manage vendors, and guarantee flawless execution from start to finish.

9
Post-Event Support

Assistance with follow-ups, media delivery, and recap materials to extend the impact of your corporate event even after it ends.
