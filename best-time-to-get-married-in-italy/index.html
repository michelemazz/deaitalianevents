The Best Time to Get Married in Italy: A Month-by-Month Guide

November 8, 2026 · 6 min read · DEA Italian Events

Italy is beautiful all year round, but the month you choose changes everything: the light in your photos, the temperature on the dance floor, the venues that are open and even your budget. Here is a clear, month-by-month look at the best time to get married in Italy, so you can pick a date with confidence.

The short answer

If you want the safest bet, aim for late May to June or September to early October. The weather is warm without being punishing, gardens and vineyards look their best, and almost every venue is open. Everything outside those windows has its own charm, and its own trade-offs.

Month by month

Spring: April and May

Spring is green, fragrant and full of flowers. The lake gardens around Lake Como and Lake Maggiore bloom from April, and the Tuscan countryside is at its most lush. Expect mild days, cooler evenings and the occasional shower, so a covered or indoor alternative is a must. Easter and the long May holiday weekends are busy, so watch those dates.

Early summer: June

June is many planners’ favourite month. Days are long, with golden light well into the evening, the countryside is still green and the sea is warming up. It is popular for weddings, so the best venues and suppliers go early.

High summer: July and August

This is peak season on the coast and by the lakes, with beautiful sunsets and late dinners outdoors. Inland areas such as Tuscany, Umbria, Rome and Puglia can get very hot, so schedule the ceremony for late afternoon and plan shade, water and cooling for guests. Around Ferragosto (15 August) many Italians are on holiday and some suppliers are unavailable.

Early autumn: September and early October

For many couples this is the best of all. The sea is still warm, the heat has eased and the light turns soft and golden. In wine regions such as Tuscany and Langhe & Roero the grape harvest adds a layer of atmosphere that photographs beautifully. Early October is also the start of truffle season in Piedmont.

Late autumn: late October and November

Moodier and more intimate. You can find good availability and a cosy, candlelit feel, but rain is more likely and some seasonal venues start to close. Venice can experience high water in autumn, which is worth keeping in mind for lagoon weddings.

Winter: December to March

Winter is ideal for small, elegant celebrations in cities and historic palaces, or for a snowy mountain wedding. Seaside and lake venues are often closed, days are short and the weather is less predictable, but you will enjoy a quieter Italy and, often, more flexibility.

Best months by destination

  • Amalfi Coast: May, June and September to early October
  • Lake Como & Lake Maggiore: late April to June, and September
  • Tuscany: late May, June, September and October
  • Langhe & Roero: September and October for the harvest, plus May and June
  • Puglia: May, June, September and October, which are cooler than peak summer
  • Rome, Florence and Venice: April to June and September to October

Practical tips for choosing your date

  • Book early. For peak months and popular Saturdays, 12 to 18 months ahead is a sensible target for venues and key suppliers.
  • Consider a Friday or Sunday. Non-Saturday dates can widen your choice of venues and ease your budget.
  • Check local holidays and events. Public holidays, festivals and sporting events affect availability and travel costs.
  • Always have a plan B. A covered terrace, a tent or an indoor room makes a rainy day stress-free.
  • Think about your guests. Flights, heat and school holidays all matter when people are travelling from abroad.
  • Check the paperwork early. Requirements for a legal or symbolic ceremony depend on your nationality and the town hall involved, so ask your planner before you fix the date.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best month to get married in Italy?

For most couples, late May, June and September are the sweet spot: warm, reliable weather, long evenings and venues at their best. Early October is a favourite for vineyard weddings during the harvest.

Is August a good time for a wedding in Italy?

It can work on the coast, by the lakes or in the mountains, but it is the hottest month inland and Italy slows down around Ferragosto (15 August). Many suppliers are on holiday and prices for accommodation are at their highest, so book very early.

How far in advance should we book an Italian wedding?

For peak months and popular Saturdays, 12 to 18 months ahead is a sensible target for venues and key suppliers. Off-peak dates, weekdays and smaller weddings are often easier to secure with less notice.

Can we get married in Italy in winter?

Yes. Winter weddings in cities such as Rome, Florence and Venice are intimate and atmospheric, and in the Alps they can be spectacular. Be aware that many seaside and lake venues close for the season.
